Codebase Inspection Executive Summary

abcg_project/questions/views.py

The entire business logic of the aBCG study intake, eligibility screening, clinical classification, and record persistence is implemented centrally within registration view in abcg_project/questions/views.py (lines 335 to 1530). Data models Participant, TptIndividual, and IneligibleIndividual in questions/models.py act as the multi-table persistence layer.

Key Architectural Strengths Verified

  • Multi-cohort routing: Eligible individuals, TPT participants, and ineligibles are stored in appropriate isolated tables.
  • Consent enforcement: Frontend immediately blocks data collection and backend cleans clinical fields when consent is "No".
  • Audit logging: Every run of the eligibility engine prints an audit trace with the assigned status and detailed reasons.
  • Non-destructive location resolution: resolve_operational_location() preserves participant geography and prevents generic 'GLOBAL' overwrites.

Key Priority & Logic Risks Identified

  • Premature duration exclusion: Check #6 (>30d pending) executes before Check #7 (Case). A confirmed NAAT+ case with a pending CXR after 30 days is classified as Excluded.
  • Premature TPT+BCG override: Check #4 (TPT+BCG) executes before Check #7 (Case). TPT recipients who develop active TB are classified as TPT+BCG instead of Case.
  • Incomplete BCG suppresses Case: Check #3 (Pending BCG) executes before Check #7 (Case).
  • Asymptomatics cannot be Controls without NAAT: Untested asymptomatic participants remain indefinitely in Pending.

Informed Consent Business Rules

Consent = "Yes"

Frontend allows wizard to proceed to Step 5 (PTB Screening). Backend proceeds with clinical assessment.

Consent = "No"

Frontend displays "Excluded from Study: Informed consent not provided" modal and halts data collection immediately upon confirmation. Backend sets classification="Excluded", clears all clinical fields, and saves to Participant.

Consent Missing / Empty

Frontend JavaScript marks the input as required and prevents stepping forward. On the backend, if an API or direct POST has empty consent, consent_declined evaluates to False and evaluation proceeds.

High-Risk Group (HRG) Eligibility Rules

Participant must satisfy both: has_hrg (any of the 6 target HRGs) AND eligible_bcg_campaign (answered 'Yes' and selected at least one criteria).

  • Elderly: Age >= 60 or checked in risk factors.
  • Malnourished: BMI < 18 kg/m² or checked in risk factors.
  • Close Contact: Household/close contact of index TB patient since 2021.
  • Diabetes: Self-reported diabetes mellitus during campaign.
  • Past TB: History of TB disease episode in past 5 years (2019-2024).
  • Smoker: History of tobacco smoking (current or past).
Ineligible Handling: If either condition fails, the participant is immediately saved as IneligibleIndividual and deleted from Participant to keep the study cohort clean.

Potential Business Rule Problems & Logic Inconsistencies (6)

Every identified issue with severity, actual behavior, source lines, example trigger, and recommended fix.

PRB-01 · HIGH SEVERITY abcg_project/questions/views.py#L1085-L1089

Check #6 (>30-day Pending Exclusion) Overrules Confirmed Microbiological TB Cases

Actual Behavior: Check #6 evaluates (now - created_at).days > 30 and (mctb == 'PENDING' or cxr == 'PENDING' or eptb_status == 'PENDING') before Check #7 (Case). If a participant is NAAT Positive (a confirmed TB Case), but their CXR was delayed or pending for >30 days, they are classified as `Excluded`.

Example Input: mctb="POSITIVE", cxr="PENDING", created_at=45 days ago → Actual Output: "Excluded" (Expected: "Case")

Recommended Fix: Check #7 (Case) should be evaluated before Check #6 (Overdue Investigation Exclusion). A confirmed positive TB diagnosis must never be discarded due to pending secondary tests.

PRB-02 · HIGH SEVERITY abcg_project/questions/views.py#L1080-L1089

Check #4 (TPT+BCG) Preempts Active TB Case Classification

Actual Behavior: Check #4 evaluates is_tpt_bcg before Check #7 (Case). If a participant who underwent TPT and received BCG develops active TB and tests NAAT positive, they are saved as classification = "TPT+BCG" in the TptIndividual table, rather than being identified as a TB Case in the primary surveillance cohort.

Example Input: tpt_undergone="Yes", bcg_status="Yes", mctb="POSITIVE" → Actual Output: "TPT+BCG" (Expected: "Case" with TPT exploratory flag)

Recommended Fix: Clarify with protocol whether TPT recipients who develop active TB should be classified as Cases within the TPT cohort or have dual reporting.

PRB-03 · MEDIUM SEVERITY abcg_project/questions/views.py#L1076-L1079

Check #3 (Incomplete BCG Details) Suppresses Active TB Case Detection

Actual Behavior: If a nurse records bcg_status="Yes" but leaves the BCG batch number or facility blank, bcg_complete evaluates to False. Because Check #3 is before Check #7, a participant with a positive sputum NAAT is classified as `Pending` ("One or more required BCG verification or vaccine details are incomplete") instead of a confirmed `Case`.

Example Input: mctb="POSITIVE", bcg_status="Yes", bcg_batch_number="" → Actual Output: "Pending" (Expected: "Case")

Recommended Fix: Disease status (Case) should be evaluated first, with incomplete BCG tracked as a verification sub-flag.

PRB-04 · MEDIUM SEVERITY abcg_project/questions/views.py#L977, L1103

Untested Asymptomatic Participants Can Never Qualify as Controls

Actual Behavior: For an asymptomatic participant who does not undergo sputum testing (undergone_testing == "No"), line 977 assigns mctb = "PENDING". Check #8 requires mctb == "NEGATIVE" to become a Control. Therefore, asymptomatic participants with no sputum test remain indefinitely in `Pending`.

Example Input: is_asymptomatic=True, cxr="NORMAL", undergone_testing="No" → Actual Output: "Pending"

Protocol Confirmation Needed: In the study protocol, are asymptomatic community controls required to submit sputum for NAAT, or does asymptomatic status with normal/absent CXR qualify as a Control?

PRB-05 · LOW SEVERITY abcg_project/questions/views.py#L4424, L4750

Orphaned "Adjudication Pending" & "Needs Correction" Dashboard Filters

Actual Behavior: The admin adjudication dashboard queries classification="Adjudication Pending" and classification="Needs Correction", but neither state is part of Participant.CLASSIFICATION_CHOICES or assigned anywhere in the codebase. As a result, the adjudication queue always falls back to mock data.

Recommended Fix: Either implement the supervisor adjudication transition workflow or decouple adjudication from the primary classification field.

PRB-06 · LOW SEVERITY abcg_project/questions/views.py#L1085

Duration Calculation Uses Record Creation Date Instead of Clinical Enrollment Date

Actual Behavior: The 30-day pending exclusion rule computes (timezone.now() - participant.created_at).days > 30. If an existing record was imported or created weeks before actual clinical intake, the duration clock starts prematurely from database insert timestamp rather than participant.date_enroll.

Recommended Fix: Use participant.date_enroll as the clinical reference date for pending duration calculations.
